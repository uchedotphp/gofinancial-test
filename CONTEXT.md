# uchedotphp

Content platform for articles, authors, and comments.

## Language

**Article**:
A published piece of writing with a title and body.
_Avoid_: Post

**Author**:
A person who writes Articles.
_Avoid_: User

**Comment**:
A public response on an Article.

**Session**:
The signed-in Author for this browser.

**Guest**:
A visitor with no Session.

**Studio**:
The protected area for a Session.

**Bookmark**:
An Author’s saved pointer to an Article, stored on the device.

**Feed Query**:
The article list filters in the URL: `q`, `page`, and optional `author`.
